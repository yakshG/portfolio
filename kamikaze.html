OVERVIEW

Kamikaze is a 2-4 player local split-screen shooter. Players fight in 3-minute matches across four maps, using a modular weapon system - knife, pistol, rifle - with highest score winning.

As Lead Programmer, I onboarded two new developers and assigned them feature work built on top of the systems I'd already set up.

CONTRIBUTIONS

Core Gameplay Systems

  • Centralized all match flow inside GameMode:
    • Score tracking per player.
    • Timer management with countdown display.
    • Win condition logic (highest score at match end).
    • Randomized respawn handling.
  • Built health system with damage detection and death handling.

Local Multiplayer

POST MORTEM

Challenge: Pawn Possession Timing in Local Multiplayer

Problem: Non-default PlayerControllers were failing to properly possess spawned pawns due to incorrect initialization in Unreal's actor lifecycle.

Solution: Traced Unreal's actor lifecycle flow and implemented possession inside a post-initialization call to ensure the correct order:

  • PlayerController initialization
  • Pawn spawning
  • Possession execution

This ensured all players could reliably control their characters.
