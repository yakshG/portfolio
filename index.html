BACKGROUND

I have a B.Tech in Computer Science Engineering and an MFA in Interactive Media from Clark University.

What has always pulled me into programming is a simple question: how did they make that? I love reverse-engineering mechanics and figuring out the underlying systems that hold a game together. Physics, in particular, draws me in. For my MFA capstone, I engineered black hole and white hole mechanics to explore this, finding it incredibly satisfying to take real-world concepts and make a player feel them through a controller.

That same curiosity pushes me to understand the systems I work with - whether that means writing a C++ multiplayer subsystem, diving into profiling tools to hunt down a dropped frame, or learning OpenGL on the side.

I also found a love for teaching along the way. Explaining systems while supporting a university rigging course or co-designing learning experiences for middle schoolers forces a kind of structural clarity that ultimately makes me a better programmer.

When I'm not in Unreal, I'm usually losing games of chess, on a racquetball court, or putting too many hours into Valorant. I'm looking for opportunities to build real systems with people who genuinely care about how things are made.
